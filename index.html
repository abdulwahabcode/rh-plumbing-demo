What We Do

Everyday plumbing, sorted.

Big job or small, it gets the same care. Here is what RH Plumbing is called out for most around Solihull and Birmingham.

Repairs & Leaks

Dripping taps, leaking pipes, running toilets and faulty flushes. We find the cause, fix it properly and make sure it stays fixed.

Bathroom Installations

Full bathroom installations and refits — suites, showers, baths and all the pipework behind them, fitted neatly and tested before we leave.

Radiators & Heating Plumbing

New radiators, replacements and heating pipework. Cold spots, noisy pipes or a radiator that never warms up — we will get the heat moving again.

Blocked Drains & Toilets

Slow sinks, blocked toilets and drains that will not shift. We clear the blockage and check the flow before calling it done.

Taps & Kitchen Plumbing

New taps fitted, sinks installed, and dishwashers and washing machines plumbed in properly — no leaks hiding under the units.
